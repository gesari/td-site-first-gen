Orienting Oneself

Now that we have a budding appreciation for the time and place in which we find ourselves, let us turn our attention inward for a moment. While much of this may seem obvious to those raised in our tradition, the purposeful degradation of society by the Chorus on Keshal and the Ylin Court on Antaris must be remediated. For those of us still coming to grips with the rapid changes our society is undergoing: there is always a place for you in the Legion.

Where You Fit In

However you came to be here, it was almost certainly facilitated by your court Court. Meant to function as an extended family, Courts strive to generate the feeling of belonging one might normally associate from being a community member of a small town where everyone knows each other.

Like any extended family, everyone will have different areas of interest, and so while many of your peers are continuing their studies at home you have found your way here. Where you go from here will be entirely up to you. Whether you return and take a position in your Court or feel called to fulfill the more specific missions of a Guild or Order, the relationship between these organizations is symbiotic.

For those uninterested or unsure of these paths, Optios will serve as an excellent source of direction. Every arcology has at least one Optio-run establishment from which they facilitate business, and even those who have not risen far enough to reach their personal notice can always find work offered by their associates who facilitate more minor matters.

The Path of Awakening

There is more to education and training than simply producing a useful servant to the Legion; devotion and practice are personal means of enrichment as well. There is a profound inner peace to be found in discipline, but more than that: there is potency. This practiced cultivation of self is referred to as the Path of Awakening, and though your progress along it will always be a personal matter, it will also be measured and recorded by the Legion.

Awakening is an arcane process, more aptly captured by poetry than mechanistic detail. The Harmonic Scales refer to it as the means by which the boundary between dreamer and Dream disappears. Lucidity, the culmination of this process, is typically associated with immortality: its most popular benefit. Those who have achieved Lucidity, however, typically suggest it is the least of their gifts.

Despite its relative rarity, the road is well-trod by this point; the hallmarks of achievement along the Path are well understood and documented. There are six plateaus in advancement that seem universal to all entities, colloquially referred to as Spell Tiers, which are cited as the purest indicator of a dreamer's advancement.

Achieving the first Spell Tier is generally considered a right of passage to adulthood, and marks a dreamer's graduation from their Academy's School of Style and formal candidacy for specializing in one of its nine Colleges. Honorary titles are bestowed upon achievement of the Fourth and Sixth tiers: Awakened and Lucid, respectively.

All positions within the Legion carry a requisite Spell Tier for applicants, but most dreamers never progress past the second, and so are scaled accordingly. Those who wish to push themselves further, however, are offered ample support.

Advancement in one's chosen Specialization is intended to provide a pathway to guide one's practice of Awakening. The techniques taught to the students of each College are meant to bridge the gap between Spell Tiers, and mastery of all ten techniques of any College is a momentous achievement.

Courts, Guilds, and Orders

Prolonged life in the Academy is not for most of us, so how do we continue to advance in our studies? Generally through application and refinement. Whether at your home Court or another, or acting on behalf of a Guild or Order, there is more than enough work to go around right now. No matter what your inclinations, there are any number of tasks which can fulfill the dual purposes of providing care for the Legion and offering you opportunities for personal growth.

Though their functions vary slightly, all three types of organization derive their authority and legitimacy from the Legion.

Courts are public organizations and serve as the basic geopolitical unit of the Dreamscape: the bureaucratic template which influences administrations for arcologies, stations, realms, and the like. While the interpretation and ultimate implementation of this structure may vary between Courts, they will generally be led by a council of ministers chaired by an Executor, a first among equals.

Guilds manage material and social concerns, especially those that fall outside the purview of a single Court's jurisdiction: economic interests, artistic collectives, academic societies, and the like. The Sculptors Guild, for instance, manages the cultivation, harvest, and distribution of aurion between Courts and other organizations as an impartial conductor of business.

Orders discharge sacred duties, generally in devotion to an ideal or an ascendant. Given its very public resurrection in recent years, everyone should recognize the example of the Censorate. Operating outside of the jurisdiction of Courts and Guilds, the Censorate is charged with overseeing the actions of the Legion's public organizations to prevent corruption and ensure that they uphold their mandates.

The Role of Optios

Since the Censorate was abolished or otherwise inactive in most areas during the Ostracism, many of you will be unfamiliar with the most public-facing agents of their order: the Optios. Appointed by a Court's Ministry of Rites, Optios are chosen from among members of the local Censorate to serve an official position in the Court.

Their primary purpose is as intermediaries between the bureacracy of the Courts and the people to whom they administer. They ensure people who operate their own private enterprises (e.g. restaurants, merchants, freelancers, etc.,) have access to what they need in the form of allocating material goods, acting as neutral intermediaries for negotiations, and offering jobs of their own when they deem necessary.

The local Censors do not generally act on their own accord: they raise concerns they find to Optios, who then determine whether to have the Censor continue with their investigation or to contract it out to someone else. Because Optios are the only members of the Censorate who operate within the bounds of the Court they oversee, they are trusted to have a broader perspective on the issues brought to them, and are empowered to make decisions as such.

Every Optio has an establishment from which they conduct their business. For those who have yet to find their place, are looking for a change of pace, or simply prefer the variability, these establishments offer a wide variety of opportunities for advancement.
