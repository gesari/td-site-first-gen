Key Points Upon Arrival

Welcome, dreamer, to Sho'ken: the Oracle Archives. By Thymoran reckoning, it is the 3545th year of the Charted Eras: a little over a decade since the Revenant Order restored the Archon Jian to the Seat of Dreams.

Within awaits the amassed knowledge and wisdom of Thymora: over three millennia of lessons, stories, theorems, art, arcanetic schematics, spell architectures, and more all distilled into a single collection to be freely shared with all members of the Legion such as yourself. Your arrival here is not unexpected: now that an Archon once again guides our steps from the Jade Tower, and the Twilight Council guard the paths between us and the realms of the Dream, you are among many looking to carry the knowledge of the past into the present.

Before you delve deeper into our mysteries, take a moment to review the following points. Though some may seem obvious to the well-educated, the context they provide to the rest of our collection is invaluable.

Pervasive Magic

As has been the case with many things, understanding our place within the Dreamscape was born from an obsession with the night sky. The neon-pastel aurorae flowing throughout space captured our ancestors' imagination such that it was not long before they began seeing its reflection all around them in microcosm: everything is suffused in the celestial Azaian Flow.

The dragons of Zjen Meng gave the name azai to the flowing aspect of the Dream, and it is understood to be the essence of magic throughout the Dreamscape. While the currents of the Azaian Flow are its most notable manifestation, ambient azai permeates the entirety of the Dream: it animates all living entities, serves as the energy which powers all of our technologies, and all dreamers can learn to channel it to produce magical effects.

The Azonai

This space we inhabit is called the Dreamscape, but do you know why that is? Dreams necessitate a dreamer, and this is why we call ourselves such, but the Dreamscape does not issue from us; quite the opposite, in fact.

Prolonged obsession with the mysteries of flowing azai and its static counterpart, azon, eventually led our ancestors to one incontrovertible conclusion: the Dreamscape has a will of its own, a numinous wellspring from which all arises. This ultimate fundament, called the Azonai, is implied rather than observed directly, and yet it makes up the totality of existence.

To most of us, this is just a known bit of cosmology that doesn't weigh heavily on our days or minds, nor does it need to. Whether we are conscious of it or not, the numinous heart of the world beats in our own chest, and through our actions comes to understand and refine itself.

Aurion

As dual reflections of the same fundament, it is important to understand that azon and azai are essentially the same type of energy with different behaviors: azon is energy that stays static, azai is energy that flows, and there are many mechanisms which can drive the conversion between the two. The most practical is the crafting of spell architecture, but the natural formation of aurion is thought of as the most symbolic.

Though its coloration varies based on where it forms, all aurion has a texture and appearance akin to a semi-translucent marble, but close inspection will prove that the glowing veins of the aurion move, charting internal currents of azaian energy that naturally replenish. The great gift of the dragon Azyra was the secret of how to rearrange the internal currents of azai within aurion: a process which allows it to permanently retain an enchantment.

Aurion crystallizes naturally in areas rich in ambient azai, and its cultivation and extraction are heavily regulated.

Arcanotech

Azaian magic is powerful, but rather ephemeral: for anyone less than a fully Lucid dreamer, enchantments must be actively channeled in order for them to persist. The advent of embedding azonian architecture inside of aurion (and, later, onai) led to the entire restructuring of society and rapid advancement of knowledge.

It began with foci: seemingly overnight, every dreamer had the ability to craft reliable architecture. Shortly thereafter, unitools expanded the powers of our telepathy to speak and collaborate across entire realms, and access plinths provided a means for unitool bearers to interface universally with advanced systems. Talismans such as invisibility cloaks began to augment our own talents, even as arcanetics began to augment our bodies and replace lost limbs. We built ships capable of traversing the sea of stars, and then the Realm Gates to let us do so in a single step.

As you well know, we Thymorans believe that, with proper study and inquiry, there is no problem that cannot be mitigated and/or solved with cogent application of azaian magics, and that arcanotech is the means by which these solutions can be made accessible to everyone.

The Harmonic Path

The cultural impact that came from understanding our universally shared origin in the Azonai was at least as profound as the technological. Developed in direct opposition to the Ruby Broodmother's dreams of draconic hegemony, The Harmonic Path stressed shared stewardship of the Flow.

A philosophical framework dictated by the dragon sage Oroxys based on the belief that entities should live in harmony with the Dream around them, it stresses adherence to the three virtues: compassion, consideration, and humility. The Harmonic Path is the closest thing most of us have to a religion. In this case, however, the numinous is found everywhere, everyday, and an entity's experience of it is promoted via mastery of self, care towards others, and responsible stewardship of the Dream.

You are most likely familiar with this already thanks to the ubiquitous book of verses, Harmonic Scales. It is the most prolific treatise ever published, and the most often sourced from our archives prior to the Ostracism.

Thymora, the Somnial Archonate

Most first-time visitors do not realize that much of the basis for Thymoran knowledge and philosophy has draconic origins. After all, it was Verin, a dreamer, who first united the disparate factions of Keshal and Zjen Meng to become Archon.

These days, Thymora is often associated with a yearning for unity and security, especially given the abrupt collapse of the Gate Network, the power vacuum created by the absence of the Seat of Dreams, and all the opportunistic injustices that came since. Now that the Archon and her champions have transmuted these wishes into reality and our base needs are met, it is time to remember who we are and where we came from.

Based on the teachings of the Harmonic Path, Thymora was founded as a federated society of Courts full of scholars and artists, explorers and archivists who viewed the generation, accumulation, preservation, and dissemination of knowledge as a sacred task. This archive's existence, and your presence here, is the fulfillment of that legacy, one which was offered to all the peoples of the dream we encountered. As the Gate Network grew to link hundreds of realms together, all were invited to establish an enclave within Verin'kar, the Jade Tower, and join the Legion. Even those who declined were treated with respect: our expansion has never been aggressive, but rather built on assurances of mutual aid and prosperity.

Even in the absence of a robust military tradition, our martial arts, refined by trillions of practitioners over millennia, have always proven adequate to protect us, and for much of our history we felt this was enough. The Grey Tide proved our weakness: though we were able to save ourselves and offer shelter to any refugees able to reach our borders, the other civilizations of the Dream were unable to withstand the arishaz assault. The Archon Shorei is the greatest warrior to have ever lived, but no single entity can bring salvation to all. Together, however, the Legion can accomplish anything...and has, time after time.

Courts and Arcologies

Though the realms of the Dream are home to all manner of different cultures of dreamers, some ideas, like the Harmonic Path, have become so popular as to be omnipresent. This has also been the case with courts and arcologies. Did you know that we once lived in separate structures, and that infrastructure was only placed within a narrow space connecting them? These sprawling settlements disrupted entire ecosystems, sometimes triggering cascading failures across entire realms, before the advent of arcologies.

Even the arcology you hail from, no matter how empty its current form, was once capable of self-sufficiency and full of dreamers: an entire city, comfortably housed in a single footprint. Each takes on its own unique form, suited to the environment it is placed, in accordance with the Path: hollowed mountains, floating citadels, massive trees grown into shape, and sculpted networks of subterranean caves are just the examples that come to mind.

I'm sure you have noticed the increased quality of life as your own home has been restored these last few years, especially now that the Archon has restored the Legion's power to elect and dismiss Court ministers, and the Censorate once again monitors their behavior.

Ancestral Veneration

Where does all this accumulated knowledge lead us, you may ask?

Following the example of Mother Kala, all Thymorans will, if possible, leave record of their knowledge and memories behind in death. This is most commonly facilitated by a spirit willow, a tree capable of storing the knowledge on small yujai petals: so long as they remain whole, dreamers may project their consciousness into the petals and speak to a vestige of the ancestor's spirit as though they were still alive.

Such gifts of knowledge are treated with utmost reverence, as is the legacy which promotes them: one which finds inherent virtue in many hands working together towards a common goal for the security and freedom of specialization it affords every individual. Through specialization, one can cultivate the self and advance the corpus of knowledge gifted to us by our ancestors: a foundational principle of the Legion.

The Legion

Though the Archon guides much of Thymora's policy and actions, most of its political power is invested within the Legion: the combined citizenry of every Court. All of its members are freely given any food, shelter, and clothing they require, as well as whatever education they wish to pursue in order to contribute to society. With the exception of the Archon, every Court official in Thymora is elected by the Legion's electorate.

It is not enough to simply be elected to a position: upon the Archon's return, she reinstated the prerequisite standards for being nominated to a role. You may have heard stories of this precedent from before the Ostracism: as a meritocratic apparatus whose structure was shaped by a cultural obsession with Awakening, those who would be elevated to positions of responsibility and authority must first prove their aptitude.

Well-trod by our ancestors at this point, progression along the path of Awakening cannot be faked or stumbled into without being ready, and thus serves adequately as a universal milestone.

This methodology ignores baseline characteristics such as where you are from, what family you were born to, and any natural talent you possess in favor of those you choose to hone over the course of your life as that which defines you in Thymora. There are myriad ways to cultivate oneself; which one you select is not nearly so important as how deeply you devote yourself to your chosen practice.

Advancement within the Legion does not merely confer status, but also responsibility; the Harmonic Path implores all adherents to serve their community according to the limits of their capabilities so that, together, as many needs as possible may be met.

From Ostracism to Renewal

The Harmonic Path does not generally espouse ideals of struggle against "evils", as personal failings are often framed as an opportunity for improvement. To willingly allow such shortcomings to negatively impact others through misuse of one's knowledge, authority, and power, however, is considered unacceptable and thought to distort the natural and ethical order of the Dream.

This concept was exemplified a little over two and a half centuries ago. After more than two millennia of peaceful transitions of power, a secret cabal of Thymoran ministers, the self-styled Chorus of Ancestors, attempted to wrest control of the Seat of Dreams from the Archon Ahr-Deth, Jian's predecessor. They partially succeeded: Ahr-Deth, Jian, the Dragon Chancellor Kisono, and many of their allies were trapped in a perpetual stasis they could not break. Many others were killed outright.

It failed spectacularly, however, in every other fashion: both the Gate Network which tied the realms together and the Hosts which connected them here, to the Oracle Archives, were severed unintentionally, unleashing powerful blasts of energy at every outlet. Entire arcologies, centers of commerce and culture including the Jade Tower itself, were emptied of life in near-instantaneous fashion. Since their attempted coup, Thymorans on their disparate realms have fractured into independent Courts seeking to find a way to reclaim the legacy of their ancestors. They have achieved varying degrees of success, but it was not until Jian found a way to reach beyond the bounds of her confinement on the realm Antaris and call out for champions to aid her that any were able to once again walk the paths between realms.

The rest of that story, I'm sure, is well-known to you: who among us has been unmoved by the heroics of the Revenant Order? The sentient arcanomaton, Slag, was the first visitor I had to receive since the beginning of the Ostracism, and though I understand it was among the least of his feats, his earnest company healed something in me broken by centuries of solitude. I have heard similar sentiments echoed by many. I'm sure you have as well.

There is still much to be done: the three realms brought back into the Archonate (Keshal, Zjen Meng, and Antaris) are still rebuilding, and even in the areas where renovations have completed, the local populace usually occupies less than ten percent of its maximum capacity. Even so, excitement abounds as progress continues ahead of pace. Archon Jian has proclaimed this era the Second Renewal, and has ordered an armada of envoy ships be built: soon we shall once again traverse the paths of Heaven's Eye to reconnect with our kin on other realms and, should they will it, return them to the fold.
